Support

Batty is a music player for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Import a playlist

Library › + › Import playlist, then paste a public playlist link from YouTube Music or Spotify. Songs Batty isn't sure about are shown so you can pick the right version.

Batty DJ is quiet

Check that "Talks between songs" is on in Settings, and that your phone isn't in silent mode. The DJ speaks in your device's language, or the one you pick in Settings.

A song won't play

Some artists don't allow their songs to play outside YouTube. Batty skips those and moves on.

Nothing plays at all

Check your connection. Batty keeps trying on its own and starts playing once it's back.

My library isn't on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

Mixes or Wrapped are missing

They appear once you've played about five songs: Wrapped for each week, month and year with enough listening.

Contact

Something else? Open an issue and we'll get back to you.
