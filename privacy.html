Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Batty doesn't collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics or ads from us.

What stays with you

Your saved songs, playlists and listening history live on your device and sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). We can't see them. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Batty DJ writes its lines and speaks them on your device: what it says is never sent anywhere.

What goes to other services

To find and play music, Batty talks directly to these services. They receive what they need to answer, under their own policies:

YouTube (Google)

Your searches, the songs you play, radio requests and the playlists you import from YouTube Music. Playback uses YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode. Google's privacy policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Spotify

When you import a Spotify playlist, Batty fetches that playlist's public page. Only the playlist link is sent. Spotify's privacy policy.

Song details

To show lyrics, Batty sends a song's title, artist and length to LRCLIB, a public lyrics catalogue. To mix songs that fit together, Batty DJ looks up their tempo and energy by title and artist in the public catalogues of Deezer and ReccoBeats. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

Children

Batty isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Questions: see Support.
